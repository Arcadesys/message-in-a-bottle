What you're running

Chicago has been quiet for ten years. A friend returns through a dimensional portal, the laws of physics start to crack, and city hall's paperwork leads somewhere no government was meant to go.

Recommended for three to five players over eight sessions. The GM materials include SWADE guidance, NPCs, success and failure routes, and a mystery that can survive missed clues.
